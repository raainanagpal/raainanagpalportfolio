Case Study 02

Influencer Payment OS

Automated invoice generation for influencer marketing payments.

Built in Make.com, this scenario is the invoice-generation stage of an Influencer Payment OS. It pulls approved campaign rows from a Google Sheet, calculates the agreed amount, agent commission and net payout, fills a Google Docs invoice template with the campaign and payment details, exports it as a PDF, emails it to the brand, and logs the result back to the sheet — turning a manual, back-and-forth payment process into one automated flow.

Case Study 07

Sector Map: Social & Live Commerce

Mapping India’s creator-to-commerce value chain, from engagement to profitable conversion.

A 12-slide sector map built for a Masters’ Union course, arguing that engagement alone doesn’t create profit in social and live commerce — the winners will be the players who own the full loop from creator and content through to conversion, fulfilment and repeat purchase. It maps the value chain, the key players, where the money actually gets made, the trust dynamics between creators, brands and customers, and where AI is already changing discovery, content and live-commerce support.

The value chain

Product / Brand → Creators & Content → Discovery & Engagement → Commerce Layer → Conversion → Fulfilment → Customer → Returns / RTO
